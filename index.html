02 / About LichtArts

Ideas grow when
visuals feel alive.

LichtArts is my space for illustration, graphic design, visual systems, and digital experiments — built around a love for expressive imagery and clear composition.

I like work that feels hand-crafted even when it lives on a screen: confident typography, environmental details, organic green accents, controlled motion, and a little bit of fantasy.
