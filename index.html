LichtArts is a graphic-art and digital design practice focused on expressive visuals, interfaces, game-related artwork, and visual systems that feel memorable without becoming noisy.

My approach mixes bold typography, high-contrast composition, environmental imagery, and carefully restrained interaction. Motion is used only where it adds meaning.
